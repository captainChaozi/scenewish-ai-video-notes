# SceneWish AI Crying Effect: input checklist

Template: [AI Crying Effect](https://scenewish.com/templates/ai-crying) · Home: [SceneWish](https://scenewish.com/)

The template turns one portrait into a short, quiet, tearful clip without writing a prompt.

## Photo

- One clear, front-facing adult portrait with both eyes visible.
- Face large enough to see the eyes and cheeks.
- Evenly lit; no sunglasses; no hand covering the face.
- One person in the frame.
- JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB, at least 128 x 128 px.

## Consent

Use a photo of yourself or of an adult who agreed to appear.

## Output

- 9:16 or 16:9
- 5 seconds, 720p, silent video
- Credit quote appears after you add the photo

## Good uses

Reaction and mood clips, a character beat to open or close a short story, or one beat in a trend-style edit. Add captions and music in your editor afterwards.

Expression and motion vary with your photo.
