# Reference-to-video: input limits by model

What each model on [SceneWish reference to video](https://scenewish.com/reference-to-video) accepts. Reference to video uses images (and on some models videos or audio) of a person, pet, product or style to keep them recognisable in a new scene.

| Model | Images | Optional videos | Optional audio | Length | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seedance 2.0 Fast | up to 9 | up to 3, 2–15 s each, ≤15 s total | up to 3 | 5–15 s | 480p / 720p |
| Seedance 2.5 | up to 30 | up to 10, 2–30 s each, ≤30 s total | up to 10 | 4–30 s | 480p / 720p / 1080p |
| Veo 3.1 Fast | 1–3 | – | – | 8 s fixed | 720p / 1080p / 4K |
| Kling v3 Omni | 1–7 | – | – | 3–15 s | 720p / 1080p / 4K |
| Wan 2.7 Reference | up to 5 | up to 5, 1–30 s each, ≤150 s total | up to 5 | see picker | see picker |

Seedance models need at least one image, video or audio reference.

## Choosing

- Many references (a product line, several characters): Seedance 2.5.
- Lower cost with a few references plus video/audio: Seedance 2.0 Fast.
- One to three images and a fixed 8-second clip: Veo 3.1 Fast.
- Few images, up to 4K: Kling v3 Omni.

## Reading the examples
