# Input rights and consent: which photos and media to use

A plain summary of the input rules across [SceneWish](https://scenewish.com/), from the [user guide](https://scenewish.com/user-guide) and template pages (checked 2026-11-02).

## General rule

- Upload media you **own or have permission to use**, or select compatible media from your account.
- Account access is needed for private uploads, generations and your library. You can browse examples before signing in.

## Portrait templates

Templates such as [Finger Heart](https://scenewish.com/templates/finger-heart) and [AI Crying Effect](https://scenewish.com/templates/ai-crying) ask for:

- One **adult**: yourself, a fictional adult character, or an adult who **agreed** to appear.
- A clear, well-lit face; no sunglasses or hand over the face.
- JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB, at least 128 × 128 px.

Template examples on the site are made from AI-created fictional adult portraits.

## Why it matters

- The result is a video of a real-looking person doing something they did not do. Consent is the line between a fun clip and a misleading one.
- Selecting a recipe prepares the creator; it does not submit a task or spend credits, so check your input before generating.

## Before sharing

Check the result, then add captions or music only if you have rights to them. Results vary between generations, even with the same recipe.
