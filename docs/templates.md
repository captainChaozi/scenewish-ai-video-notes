# SceneWish templates: inputs and outputs

Prepared one-photo effects on [SceneWish](https://scenewish.com/). With a template the model and motion are already set; you add one photo, choose a format and check the credit quote. Details are as shown on each template page on 2026-10-31. Index: [scenewish.com/templates](https://scenewish.com/templates).

| Template | Input photo | Output | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| [AI Crying Effect](https://scenewish.com/templates/ai-crying) | One clear, front-facing adult portrait, both eyes visible | 9:16 or 16:9, 5 s, 720p, silent | Calm expression changes into restrained tears, steady close-up |
| [Cinematic Orbit](https://scenewish.com/templates/cinematic-orbit) | Half-body or full-body adult portrait with some background depth | 9:16 or 16:9, 5 s, 720p, silent | A gentle camera arc reveals the scene around the subject |
| [Finger Heart](https://scenewish.com/templates/finger-heart) | Waist-up adult portrait, a hand visible, room in front of the chest | 9:16 or 16:9, 5 s, 720p, silent | Small finger-heart greeting and a gentle smile |
| [Pet Pilot](https://scenewish.com/templates/pet-pilot) | One dog, ears, muzzle and fur markings clearly visible | 9:16 or 16:9, 5 s, 720p, silent | The dog in a cockpit with goggles and head movement |

All templates accept JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB, at least 128 × 128 px.

## Common rules

- Use yourself, a fictional adult, or an adult who agreed to appear. One person (or one dog) in frame.
- Avoid sunglasses, hands over the face, heavy blur and distant group shots.
- Outputs are silent: add captions and music you are allowed to use in your editor.
- The recorded example on each page is one result from a fictional input, not a guarantee for your photo.

## Template or custom tool?

| You want | Use |
|---|---|
| Exactly the effect shown | The template |
| A different motion or camera move | [Image to video](https://scenewish.com/image-to-video) with your own prompt |
| A scene with no photo | [Text to video](https://scenewish.com/text-to-video) |
| To copy a movement from a video | [Motion transfer](https://scenewish.com/motion-transfer) |
