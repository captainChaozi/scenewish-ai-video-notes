# Video to Video on SceneWish: upload checklist

[Video to Video](https://scenewish.com/video-to-video) edits an existing clip from a prompt (new style, background or lighting).

Before uploading:

- Format MP4 or MOV, **3–10 seconds**, up to 200 MB.
- Trim to the moment you actually want edited.
- Describe the change, not the original content.
- The credit cost shows before you generate.

For longer results afterwards see [Extend Video](https://scenewish.com/extend-video). Home: [SceneWish](https://scenewish.com/).
